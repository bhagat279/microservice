===================================================Fault-Tolerance=======================================================
===>Fault Tolerance: In our micro-service user-service calling rating-service, and if rating-service down due to some
                     reason, then user-service will also down because it depends on rating-service, this scenario called
					 fault. we can say fault came from rating-service.
					 We need to tolerate this fault.
					

===> We have 2 way to handle fault-tolerance:1. Histrix
                                             2. resilience4j
